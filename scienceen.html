About the T&E GLOBAL Advanced Research Center

We share Taiwan’s best biotech innovations with the world through the T&E GLOBAL platform. Everyone seeks better health, yet many take the wrong path and face needless setbacks. Aging is not a disease—imbalanced nutrition is often the real root cause. When cells are nourished properly, the body can thrive without dramatic medical interventions. For more than eighteen years, T&E GLOBAL has followed leading scholars, refined our craft, and embraced the mission of protecting health through science-based wellness solutions.

Rooted in TCM, Empowered by Western Science

In 2019, the WHO incorporated Traditional Chinese Medicine into the ICD global medical framework.

T&E GLOBAL upholds the philosophy of “food and medicine from the same source, nurture with nature, TCM at the core, Western science in practice.” Our TCM doctors, pharmacists, and nutritionists co-create formulas that address health challenges from the inside out. With rigorously tested herbal blends, we gently improve the body at the cellular level and activate the body’s innate self-healing ability.
